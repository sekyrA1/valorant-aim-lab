# Audio asset sources and licenses

All files here are CC0/public domain and may be used commercially.

- UI selections, hit feedback, target pops, knife swishes, and handling foley: Kenney, Interface Sounds / Impact Sounds / RPG Audio. https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Weapon firing recordings for the other firearms (AR-15, Carl Gustav M45, Tikka, Walther PPQ, Smith & Wesson 642): The Free Firearm Sound Library, by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
  Selected samples are trimmed, downmixed, resampled to 48 kHz mono, faded, and normalized from the prepared recordings.
- Vandal report and arcade-style kill-confirm stinger: original sounds synthesized with the Web Audio API in `src/audio.js`; no third-party samples.
- Assault-rifle reload: SpringySpringo, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
- Stone footsteps: Fantozzi, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
